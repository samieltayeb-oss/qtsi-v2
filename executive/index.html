Official Contacts
  • Edmonton Unlimited Website: edmontonunlimited.com
  • Alberta Innovates Website: albertainnovates.ca
  • Alberta Innovates Email: smeprograms@albertainnovates.ca
  • Contact First: Edmonton Unlimited. They are the local gateway to Alberta Innovates funding.
Before You Apply
  • Eligibility: Edmonton Unlimited is for idea-stage to MVP. Alberta Innovates requires novel, patentable tech with economic benefits.
  • Common Mistakes: Assuming Alberta Innovates is for early-stage idea validation (they prefer novel, protectable tech). Skip straight to Alberta Innovates without local coaching.
Step-by-Step Process
  • Step 1: Book a free 30-minute introductory meeting on the Edmonton Unlimited website.
  • Step 2: Apply for specific cohorts based on the introductory meeting feedback.
  • Step 3: Build the MVP and commercialization narrative.
  • Step 4: Request a warm introduction to an Alberta Innovates Technology Development Advisor (TDA).
Executive Checklist
  • Visit Edmonton Unlimited website.
  • Book 30-minute introductory meeting.
  • Complete Workforce Innovation Business Model Canvas.
  • Attend meeting to discuss MVP validation.
Requires Live Verification: Alberta Innovates temporarily paused some programs in May 2026 for a redesign.

Official Contacts
  • Website: nrc.canada.ca/nrc-irap
  • Direct Application: Call 1-877-994-4727 (No online portal)
  • Email: info@nrc-cnrc.gc.ca
  • Contact First: The toll-free number to get assigned a local Edmonton ITA.
Before You Apply
  • Eligibility: For-profit SME in Canada developing genuine, technologically uncertain innovation.
  • Common Mistakes: Focusing on business problems (e.g., needing a website or training curriculum) instead of technical R&D challenges. Starting work before signing the agreement.
Step-by-Step Process
  • Step 1: Call 1-877-994-4727 to request an ITA assignment.
  • Step 2: Meet with the ITA to evaluate R&D capacity and project merit.
  • Step 3: Collaboratively develop a formal proposal with the ITA.
  • Step 4: Submit proposal for internal NRC review.
Executive Checklist
  • Determine if QTSI is building proprietary technology.
  • Draft IRAP Project Brief focusing strictly on technical uncertainties.
  • Call 1-877-994-4727.
  • Schedule initial ITA assessment meeting.

Customer & Market Validation

Funding agencies do not fund ideas; they fund validated market demand. Here is how QTSI proves demand.

Employer Letters of Intent (LOIs)

Written statements from companies willing to interview graduates. Proves the skills being taught are actually in demand.

Industry Advisory Board

Subject matter experts who vet the curriculum. Proves the startup is guided by industry, not working in isolation.

Pilot Employers

Companies agreeing to take the first cohort. Acts as the ultimate early adopter validation for scale-up grants.

Technology Partners

Vendors (e.g., AWS, Microsoft) providing tools for the cohort, validating the commercialization ecosystem.
