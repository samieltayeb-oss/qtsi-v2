Executive Profile

Built through setbacks. Focused on what truly matters.

Manav Chadha didn’t set out to build a career in cybersecurity—he built it one problem at a time over 20 years across North America, APAC, and EMEA. Having sat with operators on factory floors and executives in boardrooms, his core conviction remains simple: technology only matters when it moves the business, and risk only gets managed when someone decides to own it.

Through QTSI, Manav helps organizations build practical, respected security programs grounded in NIST CSF, ISO 27001, and hands-on threat and risk assessments. Author of The AI Mandate and builder of a privacy-first AI governance platform, he bridges the gap between technical reality, security controls, and board-level strategy.

Beyond commercial advisory, Manav is a dedicated servant leader. Having mentored over 500 IT professionals and newcomers to Canada, his mission extends to building digital safety literacy for 1 million people globally, volunteering with ISC², and serving on community advisory boards.
